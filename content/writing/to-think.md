To think I ever doubted in god\
That he should send down, a touch-ed figure divine\
To accompany me at worlds end\
Whose soft and tender touch should rest solely in mine\
Whose radiance opposes the damned, yet eternally brighter warms a once frozen soul\
And with deep gold hair ripened by the sun at ends, will I perpetually find his truth\
While, with gods and figure in mind, should she rest her head on burdened shoulders\
As we fall fast asleep — to god’s kingdom shall we go
