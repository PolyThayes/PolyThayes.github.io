There's a particular kind of tired that comes from being the designated
photographer on a trip. It isn't physical, exactly — though it can be that
too, if the light is good at the wrong hour and you're the one climbing
back up the hill for it. It's more that you're always half a step outside
the moment, watching it happen so you can also watch it happen through a
viewfinder.

I used to think this was a small sacrifice. Lately I think it's closer to a
trade. Everyone else in the group gets to have the afternoon. I get to have
the afternoon *and* the version of it that exists afterward — the one that
doesn't fade the way memory does, that can be looked at again in November
when the trip is a story instead of a place.

That's not nothing. It might even be the better half of the deal.
