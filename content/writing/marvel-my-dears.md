How small our lives\
How close our loves\
I love my friends everyday\
And my family each night\
That Love ticking\
Ready to explode

Years from now\
When I find you, a shrapnel of my love\
Blown across the world\
I shall take refuge in your home\
It’s large window overlooking the world\
It’s basement door built into soft rich dirt\
Even if small in frame large with your spirit

I will call it home\
As I have called you home
