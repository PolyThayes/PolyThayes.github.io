### Creation

If you want heroin\
You’ll have to come and get it\
Said God with his infallible tongue\
As he crushed a bit of the human soul

And made it crave the rush of heartbreak,\
The highs of love, and the blackness of the sea

He condemned the body to a mindless labor\
which would never bring it joy\
but let it love and let it see and let it feel the same

### Deposition

fighting against the weight of life\
I see you through the *haze*\
in dreams, in tongues, in bursts of thought\
in that actor’s face\
I heard you in that mating call\
the brushstrokes of that tree

Craving this, I’ll bear the weight\
of life and death and all\
I’d give my life for one next glimpse\
if you are at all
