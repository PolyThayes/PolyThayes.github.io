like a sudden breeze on a hot beach day\
sometimes I sit in awe;\
the way you must appreciate the sun to let the wind feel so much better\
you must appreciate life for the small things.\
and I was going —when I first started writing —to\
call you a small thing that cools me like a breeze.\
that calms me like the wind. But you’re not\
you’re this giant presence that keeps me\
and so I sit in awe that life is allowed to go so well for someone and that someone is me. And so I think you’re the sun, for I must have you to give any meaning to anything including the breeze
