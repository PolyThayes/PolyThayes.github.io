The first frame is almost never the one that gets kept. It's the one that
proves the shot is possible — that the light is doing something, that the
angle isn't wasted. Everything after it is the actual work.

I mention this mostly because it applies to more than photography, and I
suspect that's why I keep coming back to it. The first draft, the first
conversation, the first attempt at anything worth doing — they're rarely
the version anyone remembers. They're just proof that it's possible.

Worth remembering, on the days when the first frame is all you've got time
for.
