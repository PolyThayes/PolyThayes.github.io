What if—when you hit the ground\
Your ego shattered wide\
Spreading across the floor in shards\
O-ho! I need to cry

scouring to fit life back right\
A sharp piece snags your hand\
and gushing blood comes pouring forth\
coloring red the floor

With all the pieces even still\
Broken as they lay\
You dare not try to stand again\
Without a mask to face

Needing glue, you ask a friend\
who stooped down past your pride\
Piecing back together\
You\
who could not have it all

Rising now, a man again\
Seeing through the cracks:\
A world that never had appeared\
Looking through that glass
