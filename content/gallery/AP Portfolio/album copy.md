1. Identify the inquiry that guided your sustained investigation.
I explored the legacy of musical theater, not specific shows or scenes, but rather the interactions of the art. Theater’s legacy is passed down through gestures, shared performance, and fleeting moments formed before that curtain opens and stays long after it falls. I aim to capture how that theater and its people live and breathe art together. Through filmic image, I embody the practice passed down through generations. My investigation is such: to show, using photography, the musical and theatrical interactions between people, spaces, and the art itself.
2. Describe ways your sustained investigation developed through practice,experimentation, and revision.The art of theater is intertwined with practice(rehearsal), experimentation(actor choices), andrevision(director notes). My work, too, has been guided by these themes. Whether in camera,because film’s scarcity demands each frame be meaningful, or experimenting with techniques likeposterization, mimicking theatrical light of the stage, to adding noise and grain, emulating theimperfections of live theater. My investigation itself has grown, from focusing on myself alone and theater’s impact on me, to focusing on the theater world as a whole and how it hasimpacted those around it.
